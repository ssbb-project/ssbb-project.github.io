# AMP-003

## Overview
**AMP-003** is cationic antimicrobial peptide inspired by SET-M33 tetra-branched AMP. This peptide evaluated as part of the  
**Stop Spread Bad Bugs (SSBB)** MSCA consortium. The peptide was tested against several clinically relevant Gram-negative organisms.

---

## 🧬 Peptide Sequence
RKIRVRLSA

A tetra-branched synthetic peptide.

---

## 🦠 Target Organisms Tested

AMP-003 was evaluated against the following bacterial strains:

- **Escherichia coli** TG1  
- **Pseudomonas aeruginosa** PAO-1  
- **Klebsiella pneumoniae**

---

## 📉 MIC (Minimum Inhibitory Concentration) Results

| Organism                     | MIC Value | Units | Notes |
|-----------------------------|-----------|-------|-------|
| *P. aeruginosa* PAO-1       | **1.5**   | uM | activity observed |
| *E. coli* TG1               | **1.5**    | uM | activity |
| *K. pneumoniae*             | **6**    | uM | activity |

The MIC results reflect measurements performed under standardized SSBB experimental conditions.
